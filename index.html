Our Mission

Mission and vision

Mission

To equip people to be sober-minded and watchful: trained to recognize danger early, respond with discipline, and protect the people entrusted to their care.

Vision

Every family, congregation, and workplace in our community with someone prepared to stand watch.

  • Sober

    Clear thinking and sound judgment under pressure.

  • Vigilant

    Awareness that sees the threat before it arrives.

  • Steadfast

    Ready to stand firm and protect others.

How we train

  1. 01

    We start where you are: experience, goals, and any gaps in safety or technique.

  2. 02

    Build

    Structured drills lock in fundamentals until they hold up without conscious thought.

  3. 03
